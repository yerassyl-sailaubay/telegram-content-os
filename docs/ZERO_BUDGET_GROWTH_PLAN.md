# Zero-Budget Growth Plan

Last updated: 2026-04-02 (Asia/Almaty)

## Purpose

This is the execution plan for getting Telegram Content OS to first real revenue without spending money.

This is **not** a branding document, a long-term GTM deck, or a list of clever ideas.

This document exists to answer one question:

> What should I do next that gets me closer to money?

## The Rule Set

Before doing any task, ask:

1. Will this get me more qualified conversations?
2. Will this get me more signups?
3. Will this get me more activated users?
4. Will this get me more paid users?

If the answer is **no** to all four, do not do it now.

## What the Product Can Truthfully Sell Right Now

Safe product story:

- Connect Telegram channels
- Ingest Telegram posts
- Capture ideas in the dashboard
- Generate drafts from ideas, YouTube links, and article URLs
- Repurpose existing content
- Schedule and publish to Telegram
- Review Telegram analytics

This is the strongest truthful wedge:

> Telegram-first content workflow for serious creators who publish often.

## What Not to Sell Right Now

Do **not** lead with these until they are actually shipped and productized:

- Telegram DM inbox as a creator capture workflow
- Voice-note to draft workflows
- Podcast ingestion
- "AI learns your voice" language
- Fully mature multi-platform suite messaging
- Closed-loop autonomous analytics claims

## Best First Customer

Start narrow.

Best first customer profile:

- Russian-speaking or CIS-based creator/operator
- Publishes text-heavy expert or commentary content
- Publishes at least 3 times per week
- Already has a Telegram channel with posting history
- Often turns sources or rough ideas into posts
- Wants consistency more than hype
- Can pay with the currently available billing setup

Best sub-segment for first paid conversions:

- creators or operators managing **2-5 channels**, or
- creators who will quickly outgrow the Free plan limits

Why this segment first:

- They understand the problem immediately
- They can get value from the product as it exists today
- They are more likely to pay for Plus instead of treating Free as a toy

## Positioning

Use one positioning line everywhere.

Primary line:

> Turn Telegram posts, ideas, YouTube videos, and articles into publishable Telegram content in your own style — then schedule, publish, and track it in one place.

Short version:

> Telegram-first content OS for creators who publish seriously.

Messaging pillars:

1. **Telegram-first** — not another generic social suite
2. **Source to draft** — video, article, idea, old post -> new post
3. **Keep your voice** — guided by channel profile, not generic AI sludge
4. **Operational workflow** — draft, schedule, publish, analytics in one place

## The Revenue Thesis

Do not optimize for scale first.

Optimize for this sequence:

1. Get the right person into the product
2. Get them to first useful draft or first publish
3. Get them to hit a real limit or regular use case
4. Convert them to Plus

Current pricing truth:

| ---- | --------- | ---------------- | ------------------- | ----------------- |
| Free | $0        | 10               | 5                   | 1                 |
| Plus | $19/month | 100              | 50                  | 5                 |

For zero-budget growth, **Plus** is the main target.

Why Plus first:

- More AI calls is a clean upgrade reason

## What Success Looks Like in the Next 30 Days

You do not need hundreds of users yet.

You need:

- 50 highly relevant outreach attempts
- 10 qualified signups
- 5 activated users
- 2-3 paid users
- a clear pattern for why they paid

That is enough to learn what the real business is.

## The Only Funnel That Matters

Track this funnel and nothing else until it is healthy:

1. Qualified visitor or conversation
2. Signup
3. First value
4. First publish or first scheduled post
5. Paid conversion

For this product, **first value** means one of:

- generated one useful draft
- repurposed one useful post
- scheduled or published one useful post

Seeing the dashboard is **not** first value.

## Priority Order

Do the work in this order:

1. Fix trust leaks in copy and pricing
2. Improve first-run activation path
3. Run direct outreach to ideal users
4. Manually onboard the first serious users
5. Ask for payment when value becomes obvious
6. Publish proof content from what you learn
7. Repeat the winning message and audience

## Trust Fixes to Do Before Pushing Hard

Before trying to drive more traffic, make sure public messaging matches shipped reality.

Immediate fixes:

- pricing copy matches actual enforced limits
- remove unsupported bot-inbox language
- remove voice-note and podcast claims
- replace "AI learns your voice" with channel-profile-guided wording
- keep product framing Telegram-first, not all-platform-first

Reason:

Every trust leak makes all future traffic more expensive, including free traffic.

## Activation Fixes That Matter Before Scale

Cheap, high-leverage improvements:

1. Make the first action after signup obvious: **connect Telegram channel**
2. Make first-value paths clearer: **create from URL**, **capture idea**, **publish first post**
3. Make empty states useful instead of decorative
4. Add minimal funnel logging for:
   - signup
   - channel connected
   - first draft created
   - first post published or scheduled
   - upgrade
5. Tighten onboarding checklist so it reflects the real user journey

## Zero-Budget Acquisition Strategy

Your zero-budget acquisition stack is not broad content marketing.

It is:

1. founder-led direct outreach
2. public proof content in Russian
3. manual onboarding
4. lightweight partnerships and community placements

### Channel 1: Direct Outreach

This is the main channel until you get repeatable conversions.

Target places:

- personal network
- Telegram creator chats
- founder/marketing/editor communities
- niche Telegram channels where your ICP hangs out
- previous contacts who already publish seriously

Rules:

- outreach only to people with obvious fit
- use short messages
- show relevance fast
- offer help getting first value
- avoid mass-blast spam

### Channel 2: Public Proof Content

Make content that proves the product works.

Best formats:

1. source -> draft demonstration
2. before/after examples
3. repurpose one post into multiple formats
4. short case studies from real users
5. founder notes about where creators get stuck

Best platforms for now:

- your Telegram presence
- VC.ru
- Habr
- maybe VK / Yandex Zen later if you can maintain consistency

### Channel 3: Lightweight Partnerships

Look for adjacent audiences, not giant media plans.

Best partnership targets:

- Telegram analytics tools
- Telegram scheduling or utility bot owners
- creator communities
- niche channel admins with practical business/creator audiences

Offer structure:

- free access in exchange for feedback
- co-created demo or case study
- workflow comparison
- small pilot placement later if organic fit is obvious

## Channels to Ignore for Now

Do not spend time here yet:

- paid ads
- broad SEO programs
- Product Hunt as a major launch effort
- English LinkedIn/Twitter audience building
- affiliate systems
- referral programs
- rebrand work
- complex content engines
- broad agency outreach

These are usually procrastination disguised as growth.

## Message Angles to Test

Test only 3 angles at first.

### Angle A: Source -> Post

> Turn YouTube videos and articles into Telegram posts in your style.

Use when targeting creators who consume lots of content.

### Angle B: Never Run Out of Content

> Stop staring at an empty Telegram content calendar.

Use when targeting creators struggling with consistency.

### Angle C: Keep Your Voice, Cut the Effort

> Draft faster without sounding like generic AI.

Use when targeting quality-sensitive writers and expert channels.

Do not test 15 angles. Test 3. Keep the winner.

## Outreach System

### Daily quota

Every weekday:

- send 10 targeted outreach messages
- publish 1 proof-based content piece or snippet
- run 2 onboarding conversations if you have interested users
- fix 1 bottleneck in the funnel

### Outreach criteria

Only message people who match at least 4 of these:

- active Telegram creator/operator
- text-heavy posts
- expert or commentary content
- consistent posting cadence
- obvious content pressure
- uses YouTube/articles/ideas as source material
- likely to benefit from scheduling + publishing flow

### Outreach message structure

1. specific observation about their channel
2. short statement of relevant outcome
3. soft offer to try the product
4. low-friction next step

Example:

```text
Привет. Посмотрел ваш канал — у вас сильный формат [specific thing].

Я делаю инструмент, который превращает YouTube/статьи/идеи в черновики постов для Telegram в стиле канала, а потом помогает их запланировать и опубликовать.

Если хотите, дам доступ и помогу сделать первый пост на ваших материалах. Бесплатно, мне нужен быстрый честный фидбек.
```

Rules:

- do not send essays
- do not oversell
- do not pretend it does more than it does
- do not ask for payment in the first message

## The 30-Day Plan

## Week 1 — Make the Funnel Honest

Goal:

- remove trust leaks
- make first-run path clearer
- prepare for outreach

Tasks:

- fix pricing copy to match reality
- remove unsupported public claims
- tighten positioning to one Telegram-first message
- define activation event clearly
- make a simple tracking sheet
- build a list of 50 target people/channels/communities
- send first 20 outreach messages

Required output by end of week:

- honest public narrative
- one clear positioning line
- tracking sheet live
- 20 outreach attempts done
- 3 real user conversations completed

## Week 2 — Get the First 10 Qualified Signups

Goal:

- get serious users into the product
- observe friction live

Tasks:

- send 10 targeted messages per day
- offer temporary Plus access in exchange for feedback if needed
- help users reach first value manually
- record one short demo video
- publish 3 public proof posts this week
- collect objections in one place

Questions to ask every user:

1. What made you try it?
2. Where did you get confused?
3. What would make this worth paying for?

Required output by end of week:

- 10 qualified signups
- 5 users reaching first value
- top 3 objections identified

## Week 3 — Get First Paid Users

Goal:

- convert the users who already see value

Tasks:

- identify users who are hitting usage limits or regular workflows
- ask directly for upgrade when the fit is clear
- improve upgrade prompts where value is already proven
- publish 2 mini case studies

Upgrade triggers to use:

- user needs more than 1 channel
- user is approaching 10 AI calls
- user is publishing regularly
- user is using the product for active channel operations

Simple upgrade ask:

```text
Looks like you’re already using this for real work. Free is good for trying it, but for regular use / more channels / more AI volume, Plus is the right fit. Want me to switch you over?
```

Required output by end of week:

- first 1-3 paid users
- one clear pattern for why users pay
- one clear pattern for why they do not

## Week 4 — Double Down on What Worked

Goal:

- stop guessing
- remove low-value activity

Tasks:

- review all outreach and signup data
- identify best audience slice
- identify best message angle
- identify best content format
- identify biggest activation bottleneck
- cut at least one low-value channel or activity

Keep only:

- the best message
- the best audience
- the best proof content format
- the best onboarding path

- making many landing pages

- broad audience expansion
- chasing English credibility before local proof
- building flashy features instead of fixing conversion blockers
- doing "marketing" that creates activity but not revenue

Do these first:

1. Fix trust and pricing mismatch everywhere public
2. Send 50 highly targeted outreach messages over the next 5 days
