# Telegram Content OS — Product Features

Telegram Content OS is an AI-powered content platform for Telegram channel creators who want to grow an English-speaking audience on LinkedIn and Twitter. It handles the full workflow: ingesting posts from Telegram, adapting them for each platform's audience and tone, scheduling, publishing, and measuring results — all from one place.

---

## Telegram Channel Integration

Connect any Telegram channel in minutes using the built-in bot setup. Once connected, every new post is automatically imported into your content library the moment it's published. Text, images, videos, and documents are all captured and ready to work with.

For channels that want to engage new subscribers from the start, welcome message templates let you greet new members automatically with personalized messages that include their name, the channel name, or any custom text you choose.

---

## AI-Powered Content Adaptation

This is the core of the product. Raw Telegram posts (typically written in Russian, in a conversational channel style) go through a two-step AI pipeline before they ever reach LinkedIn or Twitter.

The first step is a clean, accurate translation. The second step is cultural and platform adaptation: the AI rewrites the content to match the professional tone LinkedIn audiences expect, or the punchy, hashtagged style that performs on Twitter. These aren't interchangeable outputs. Each platform gets content that feels native to it.

The AI also builds a tone profile of your channel by analyzing your post history. That means the adapted content sounds like you, not like a generic translation tool.

For Twitter specifically, long posts are automatically split into threads with smart sentence-boundary detection, so threads read naturally rather than cutting off mid-thought.

You can choose which AI model powers your adaptations: GPT-4.1 mini, Claude Haiku, or let the platform pick the most cost-effective option automatically.

---

## Cross-Platform Publishing

Connect your LinkedIn and Twitter/X accounts once, then publish to either or both from inside the platform.

Before anything goes live, a side-by-side preview shows you the original Telegram post alongside the LinkedIn and Twitter versions. You can edit any version before publishing. When you're ready, post to one platform or broadcast to all connected platforms simultaneously with a single action.

---

## Content and Media Library

Every imported Telegram post lives in a searchable archive. Filter by date, content type, channel, or publication status to find what you need quickly. Status labels (draft, adapted, published) make it easy to see where each piece of content stands in your workflow.

Media files (images, videos, documents) are stored with thumbnail previews so you can browse your library visually, not just by filename.

---

## Visual Scheduling

Plan your content calendar without juggling spreadsheets or third-party tools. The calendar view shows all scheduled posts across platforms, and rescheduling is as simple as moving a post to a new date.

All scheduling is timezone-aware, so posts go out at the right local time for your audience regardless of where you're working from. For content that repeats on a pattern, recurring schedules (daily, weekly, or monthly) handle the automation so you don't have to manually queue the same type of post every week.

---

## Analytics Dashboard

Track how your content performs across Telegram, LinkedIn, and Twitter from a single dashboard. Metrics include views, likes, shares, comments, and engagement rate, all pulled automatically and updated regularly.

An engagement heatmap highlights which days and times generate the most interaction, helping you find the posting schedule that works best for your specific audience. Platform comparison charts let you see at a glance which channel is performing and how your numbers trend over time.

---

## Multi-Language Interface

The full platform is available in both Russian and English. Switching languages updates the entire interface, including date and time formats that match the selected locale.

---

## Settings and Account Management

Manage your profile, timezone, and language preference from a single settings page. A connected accounts panel shows the live status of every linked platform (Telegram, LinkedIn, Twitter) and lets you disconnect any of them with one click.

AI preferences are configurable per account: choose your preferred model and set the default adaptation tone that best fits your brand voice.
